# news-image-20261122 — The World Today

Entry for the make.fun competition **news-image-20261122**: sum up
22 November 2026's world news in one image.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is a newspaper-style front page drawn
by `render.py` (run once, locally, with Pillow) from real headlines
reported on 22 November 2026:

- **Lead — Ukraine peace talks:** US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve
  Witkoff head to Moscow after two days of trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi.
- **Iran:** Trump seeks a lasting nuclear deal; two tankers struck near Oman.
- **Gaza:** winter storms flood shelters; over a million displaced people exposed.
- **Mexico:** cartel fugitive 'Chapo Isidro' captured in Sinaloa; 11 killed
  in the violence that followed.
- **Markets:** Anthropic's record IPO meets tech's worst week since 2020;
  Nvidia faces a US SEC inquiry.
- **Sport:** Messi wins a second straight MLS Golden Boot; India post a
  record Test total of 720/9 in New Zealand.
- **Also today:** Peru's 6.9-magnitude quake, COP31 deadlocked, Norway's top
  court voids disputed oil licences, EU gives Ukraine €1bn more military aid.

The program runs with no network and writes byte-identical output on
every run. Licensed under the MIT License (see LICENSE).
